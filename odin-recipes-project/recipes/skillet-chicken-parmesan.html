Skillet Chicken Parmesan

Servings: 4 Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 680g boneless, skinless chicken breast cut into 4 thin cutlets
  • 0.5 cups panko breadcrumbs
  • 0.25 cups grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup red pasta sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 225g spaghetti
  • Fresh basil, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Pound the chicken cutlets to an even ½-inch thickness. Mix the panko and Parmesan. Dip each cutlet in the egg, then coat in the breadcrumb mixture.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the cutlets 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through
  3. Spoon red sauce over each cutlet and top with mozzarella. Cover and cook 2 minutes until the cheese melts.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti. Serve the chicken over pasta and garnish with basil.

Macronutrients (per serving)

Calories: 635
Protein: 57g
Carbs: 51g
Fat: 18g
