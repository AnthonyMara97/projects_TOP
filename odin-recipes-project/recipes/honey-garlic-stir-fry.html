Honey Garlic Chicken Stir-Fry

Servings: 4 Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 680g boneless, skinless chicken breast cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 1 large carrot, thinly sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsn low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsn honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp water (slurry)
  • 3 cups cooked jasmine rice, for serving
  • Sesame seeds and sliced scallions, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, and cornstarch slurry in a small bowl. Set aside.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer, season with salt and pepper, and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the broccoli, bell pepper, and carrot for 3–4 minutes until crisp-tender. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss 1–2 minutes until glossy and thickened.
  5. Serve over rice and garnish with sesame seeds and scallions.

Macronutrients (per serving)

Calories: 480
Protein: 44g
Carbs: 50g
Fat: 9g
